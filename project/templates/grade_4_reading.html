📖 Reading Journey: Adventure Stories

The Magical Forest Adventure

Maya loved exploring the woods behind her house. One sunny morning, she discovered a shimmering path she had never seen before. Curious, she followed it deeper into the forest. Suddenly, she heard a tiny voice calling her name. "Maya, help us!" It was a family of fairies trapped under a fallen log. With determination, Maya lifted the heavy log and freed the grateful fairies. As a thank you gift, they gave her a magical seed. When Maya planted it in her garden, a beautiful rainbow tree grew overnight. Now, Maya visits the forest every day to help her fairy friends and explore new magical places.

🎯 Comprehension Quest

Quest 1: What did Maya discover in the forest?

Quest 2: Who was trapped under the log?

Quest 3: What grew from the magical seed?
