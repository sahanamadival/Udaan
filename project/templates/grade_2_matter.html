🧊 Matter & Materials

Explore solids, liquids, and gases around us

🔍 Three States of Matter

🧊

Solids

  • 🔹 Keep their shape
  • 🔹 Hard to squeeze
  • 🔹 Examples: Ice, wood, metal
💧

Liquids

  • 🔹 Take container shape
  • 🔹 Can be poured
  • 🔹 Examples: Water, milk, juice
💨

Gases

  • 🔹 No definite shape
  • 🔹 Fill any space
  • 🔹 Examples: Air, oxygen

🎨 Properties of Materials

Material Properties

📏 Hard/Soft - How firm something feels
🔥 Heavy/Light - How much it weighs
✨ Shiny/Dull - How it reflects light
🔄 Flexible/Stiff - How easily it bends

Common Materials

🪵 Wood - From trees, used for furniture
⛓️ Metal - Strong and shiny, conducts heat
🧱 Plastic - Light and can be molded
🧵 Fabric - Soft material for clothes

🔄 Changes in Matter

reversible Changes

  • ✅ Ice melting into water
  • ✅ Water freezing into ice
  • ✅ Paper being folded
  • ✅ Clay being reshaped
  • Can be changed back!

Irreversible Changes

  • ❌ Burning paper
  • ❌ Cooking an egg
  • ❌ Rusting metal
  • ❌ Baking a cake
  • Cannot be changed back!
