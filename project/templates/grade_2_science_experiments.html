Safety First!

🌱 Plant Growth Experiment

What You'll Learn:

  • • How seeds grow into plants
  • • What plants need to survive
  • • The plant life cycle

Materials Needed:

Beans or sunflower seeds, cotton balls, clear plastic cup, water

Steps:

  1. 1. Wet cotton balls with water
  2. 2. Place cotton balls in cup
  3. 3. Put 3-4 seeds between cotton and cup
  4. 4. Place in sunny spot
  5. 5. Water daily and observe growth
  6. 6. Record changes in growth chart

💧 Water Cycle Experiment

What You'll Learn:

  • • How water evaporates and condenses
  • • The water cycle in nature
  • • Where rain comes from

Materials Needed:

Clear glass jar, hot water, ice cubes, small plate

Steps:

  1. 1. Pour hot water in jar (1/4 full)
  2. 2. Place plate on top of jar
  3. 3. Add ice cubes on the plate
  4. 4. Watch water droplets form
  5. 5. Observe evaporation and condensation

🌈 Density Rainbow Experiment

What You'll Learn:

  • • How liquids have different densities
  • • Why oil floats on water
  • • Layering different materials

Materials Needed:

Honey, corn syrup, dish soap, water, oil, rubbing alcohol, food coloring, clear glass

Steps:

  1. 1. Add food coloring to each liquid
  2. 2. Pour honey first (heaviest)
  3. 3. Carefully add each liquid slowly
  4. 4. Watch layers form without mixing
  5. 5. Observe the colorful rainbow effect

🌋 Volcano Eruption Experiment

What You'll Learn:

  • • Chemical reactions between substances
  • • How real volcanoes work
  • • Gas formation and pressure

Materials Needed:

Baking soda, vinegar, dish soap, food coloring, plastic bottle, clay/playdough

Steps:

  1. 1. Make volcano shape around bottle
  2. 2. Add 2 tablespoons baking soda
  3. 3. Add few drops dish soap
  4. 4. Add red food coloring
  5. 5. Pour vinegar and watch eruption!
