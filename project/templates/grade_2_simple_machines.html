⚙️ Simple Machines

Learn about levers, wheels, and how things work

What are Simple Machines?

Simple machines help us do work more easily by changing the direction or amount of force we use.

💪
Less Force Needed
🔄
Change Direction
⚡
Work Faster

🔧 The Six Simple Machines

📏

Lever

A stiff bar that pivots around a fulcrum to lift or move things

Example: Seesaw, crowbar

螺丝

Wedge

Two inclined planes stuck together, used for cutting or splitting

Example: Knife, axe

🌀

Wheel and Axle

A wheel attached to a rod (axle) that helps things roll and move

Example: Car wheels, doorknob

🧱

Inclined Plane

A slanted surface that helps move heavy objects up or down

Example: Ramp, slide

缧

Screw

An inclined plane wrapped around a cylinder, used to hold things together

Example: Jar lid, screw

🛞

Pulley

A wheel with rope around it that changes direction of force

Example: Flag pole, well bucket

⚙️ How Simple Machines Work

Work Made Easier

1️⃣ Reduce Force - Need less strength to move heavy objects
2️⃣ Change Direction - Pull down to lift up (like a pulley)
3️⃣ Move Faster - Wheels help things roll quickly

Real World Examples

🏗️ Construction cranes use pulleys
🚗 Cars have wheel and axle systems
🔩 Door hinges work like levers
💈 Stairs are inclined planes

🔨 Simple Machines Around You

  • • Bottle opener lifting caps
  • • Nutcracker cracking nuts
  • • Tweezers picking up small things

Wheel Examples

Wheels make our world go round:

  • • Bicycle wheels for riding
  • • Car wheels for transportation
  • • Skateboard wheels for fun
  • • Doorknobs for opening doors
